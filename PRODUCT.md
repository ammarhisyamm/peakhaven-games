# PH Arcade
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Peak Haven customers aged 30–50, approximately 65% female per the user.
## Product Purpose
Mobile casual games with 18 sequential missions plus free play. Vial Tower, Vial Merge and Lab Sort. English UI, local progress, no customer account.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Coins and gems are local in-game currency, earned through play and free daily rewards. Six collectible bottle skins equip in Tower. Daily free spin and a five-game coin boost. No payment or store value. Existing Site audience stays private. Progress and personal bests migrate from the prior version.
## Brand Commitments
The five latest supplied reference images replace all prior visual authority. PH ARCADE white geometric wordmark, official Peak Haven arrow/molecule symbol, happy silver-capped glass vial mascot, 3D alpine castles and winding cyan map, collectible short glass vials, silver/blue/gold reward chests. Palette #0A1B3D #0EA5FF #38BDF8 #E6F4FF #FFD24D #F8FAFC. Extended geometric display and Inter body.
## Product Principles
Mobile first, large touch targets, short loops, satisfying motion, no forced timer, truthful local progress.

## October 10 gameplay extension
Five playable games: Vial Tower, Vial Merge, Lab Sort, Peak Blocks (8x8 block blast), Bottle Mahjong (34 layered tiles / 17 pairs). The new puzzle games are free play, while the original 18-step progression stays intact. All gameplay uses full raster environments and a navy legible HUD. No baked game board or score; boards and scores are interactive and real. Artwork preloads with progress and cancel/retry before each game. Power tools are per-run and have three uses each; no payment. Home uses a horizontally scrollable game shelf, readable left hero, 44px header controls, and a consistent five-tab nav.

## Vessel material finishing

Lab Sort composites live numbered cylindrical liquid layers beneath a transparent raster glass tube with polished chrome rim and base. Vial Merge renders the real falling/merging pile inside a transparent 3D chrome and glass chamber frame. Tower uses individually bounded proportional sprites at a larger focal size with coordinated stack spacing and camera movement. Collection indicators show checks for owned bottles, arrows for available unlocks, and locks for unavailable bottles.
