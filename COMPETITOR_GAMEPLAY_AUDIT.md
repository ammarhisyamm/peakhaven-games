# Mobile gameplay audit — 11 October 2026

Scope: review official developer descriptions and published controls for all nine genres, inspect the Peak Haven implementation, and validate the revised game flows. This is a control and interface audit; it does not claim hands-on play of every installed competitor app. Developer descriptions are evidence for mechanics, while the timing, layout and accessibility decisions below are Peak Haven implementation choices.

| Peak Haven | Primary reference | Relevant published pattern | Applied behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vial Tower | [Stack — Ketchapp](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stack/id1080487957) | Stack higher and chase a best score; simple presentation. | Retain direct arena tap/Space, perfect-drop feedback and endless difficulty; use the portrait game shell. |
| Vial Merge | [Suika Game — Aladdin X](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/suika-game-aladdin-x/id6469114836) | Finger positions the object, release drops it; equal objects merge; overflow ends the run. | Retain drag/release, keyboard positioning, visible overflow line and settling physics; constrain the camera to mobile width. |
| Lab Sort | [Water Sort Puzzle — IEC](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/water-sort-puzzle/id1514542157) | Tap source and destination; pour matching colors into available capacity; restart a stuck puzzle. | Retain matching-run pours, capacity checks, Undo/Hint/Reset and untimed levels; tubes fit the portrait shell. |
| Peak Blocks | [Block Blast — Hungry Studio](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485) | Drag and place shapes, clear rows/columns, pursue endless scores. | Add drag/drop with a preview lifted 48px above the finger; retain tap and keyboard alternatives, legal-fit preview and Undo. Cancel a drag without changing the board. |
| Bottle Mahjong | [Mahjong — MobilityWare](https://www.mobilityware.com/mahjong/) | Remove matching exposed tiles; covered tiles cannot be removed. | Retain exposed-pair rules, visibly blocked tiles, solvable hints and Undo/Shuffle. Late large boards scroll inside their arena rather than making the whole game page scroll. |
| Orbit | [Dunk Shot — Ketchapp](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dunk-shot/id1301375510) | Ball-to-hoop arcade scoring and repeat shots. | Keep the requested pull/aim/release mechanic, dotted aiming arc and descending hoop entry, with three misses and progressive difficulty. The exact aiming gesture is our interpretation of the user's brief, not a claim from the store description. |
| Ridge Run | [Flappy Bird — developer listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_SG&id=com.flappybirdfoundation.flappybird) | Tap/timing flight through pipes and obstacles. | Use immediate lift on arena tap/Space and paired bottles reaching both screen edges. The original silhouette is reinterpreted in Peak Haven materials. Preserve cap/label proportions and round bubbles; freeze each gate's gap so scoring cannot shrink an obstacle already approaching. |
| Bond | [Knife Hit — Ketchapp](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/knife-hit/id1336527043) | Time throws into a rotating target and avoid existing projectiles. | Replace the 360ms single-flight lock with 120ms flights and a 65ms debounce. A second tap may launch while the first is travelling. Keep impact-time collision checks and pause protection; initial pins leave a clear launch lane. Tune rotation from 2.1 to 4.2 radians/second with a 0.22-radian collision margin so quick, well-timed throws can land. These timings are our tuning, not measurements of Knife Hit. |
| Sequence | [Candy Crush — official controls](https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000750278-Controls-how-to-switch-and-match-candies) | Drag a candy to exchange with a neighbour; form horizontal/vertical matches within a move budget; refill from above. | Accept all four adjacent swap attempts and snap long swipes to one neighbour. Animate nonmatching attempts out and back without spending a move; matches consume one move and settle through cascades before completion. Return animation is a Peak Haven usability choice. |

Shared decisions: one portrait mobile camera, maximum 480px on desktop; edge-to-edge on phones; one 48px back/pause control; compact live HUD; no duplicate game title or launch CTA; no document scrolling during play. Loading and results use the same camera. Loading fill remains clipped inside its track with usable cancel/retry. Keep optional sound, reduced motion, keyboard alternatives and local saves.

Verification: engine tests cover repeated taps, impact collision, gate boundaries, four-way reversible swaps, long swipe/cancel, uniform coordinates, merge physics, procedural sort solvability and Mahjong solvability. Browser checks cover actual mobile/desktop geometry, loading bounds, drag/drop plus Undo/tap alternatives, direct arena controls and each game's pause/back menu. 43 automated tests passed. All nine games were checked at 320×568; portrait play and loading were also inspected at 390×844, and the centered 480px shell was checked in a 1280×800 desktop viewport. Native browser drag placement, Undo and tap placement were confirmed in Peak Blocks.
